01

Who this app is for

PandaPlate is for people aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you are under 18, some estimate screens (for example weight-date projections) may be limited or skipped for honesty and safety.

02

What PandaPlate is (and isn’t)

PandaPlate is a gentle food-logging and companion app. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis tool, and not a calorie-prescription service. BMI and similar figures, when shown, are informational only from numbers you enter.

03

Your account

Finishing setup may require Sign in with Apple so your session can stay with you. PandaPlate offers Sign in with Apple only — Google sign-in is not included. You are responsible for the account you use. Do not use someone else’s credentials.

04

Free and Premium

Free includes manual logging, water, workouts, fasting tools, and a limited number of live Food Scan recognitions per day (currently 3 per day when the scan server is connected), as offered in the build. Premium is optional and sold through Apple StoreKit only when subscription products are configured (product IDs com.charlie.pandaplate.premium.monthly and com.charlie.pandaplate.premium.yearly). Prices and renewal terms must come from the App Store purchase sheet. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple ID subscription settings.

05

Camera, photos, and Health

Camera access is requested only when you choose to take a photo for a meal log or Food Scan. Apple Health is read only if you tap Connect; we do not write to Health in this version. You can skip Health.

06

Food Scan

Live food recognition uses an owner-hosted HTTPS proxy when you choose Food Scan and give fresh consent for that upload. Selecting a photo for a manual note alone does not send the image. The current proxy may send the image to DeepSeek for vision analysis and use USDA FoodData Central for nutrient lookup from food names or identifiers (USDA does not receive your photo). DeepSeek may process data under its own policy; processing or storage could involve China. Results are editable estimates, not health advice. Barcode lookup is not offered as a production service in this version. Do not scan images of other people without permission.

Each live scan requires fresh in-app consent. If you do not want a photo processed off-device, do not consent to the scan.
07

Acceptable use

Don’t misuse the app, attempt to break scan quotas unfairly, harass others through shared exports, or use PandaPlate for anything illegal. Soft Show-Up and Pip reactions are supportive — not a grading system, heart meter, or guilt loop.

08

Session data

In this version, meals, photos, water, and preferences are primarily held in device memory for the session and may reset when the app closes. Cloud sync is not yet available. See the Privacy Policy.
